### <center><b><i>📚 GenAI Course Assistant</i></b></center>
<p>
  Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is an AI framework that combines information retrieval with text generation, allowing a large language model to fetch relevant data from a custom, external knowledge base to provide grounded, accurate, and up-to-date answers rather than relying solely on its pre-trained memory.
</p>
----

### Example Queries:
You can test the RAG model's comprehension by asking it questions based on the ingested corpus:
<ul>
  <li>What specific topics are taught in the system architecture and vector database modules?</li>
  <li>What is the total duration and expected weekly time commitment for this course?</li>
  <li>Does the program provide a verifiable certificate upon completion?</li>
  <li>Can I access the course through a free trial, and what specific modules are included?</li>
  <li>What are the technical requirements and deliverables for the final capstone project?</li>
</ul>
